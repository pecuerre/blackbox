# Emails

All emails come from "Bookclub <hello@bookclub.example>".

- **Email confirmation**, right after sign-up.
- **Password reset**, when requested from the login page.
- **Meeting reminder**, two days before each meeting, to members who said
  they're going.
- **New book of the month**, to every member of the group, when an organiser picks it.

Every email except confirmation and password reset has an unsubscribe link.
