# Bookclub

Bookclub is a small website for neighbourhood reading groups. A group picks
one book per month, members say whether they're attending the monthly meeting,
and after the meeting everyone can leave a short review of the book.

Anyone can browse the groups and the books they've read. You need an account
to join a group, RSVP to a meeting, or write a review.

The site is in English. It must work well on phones, because most people open
it from a link in a group chat.
