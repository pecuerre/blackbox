# Group page

Who can see it: everyone.

Shows the group's name, city, description, organisers, and member count.

- **This month:** the book of the month with its cover, and the next
  meeting's date, time and place. Logged-in members of the group see
  "Going" / "Not going" buttons and the list of who's going.
- **Past books:** each book the group has read, newest first, with the
  average rating from this group's reviews.
- A "Join group" button for logged-in people who aren't members yet.
