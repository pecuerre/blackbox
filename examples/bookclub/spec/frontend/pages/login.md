# Login page

Who can see it: people who are not logged in. Logged-in members who open it
are sent to the home page.

- The member logs in with their email and password.
- There is a "Remember me" checkbox. When ticked, they stay logged in for 30 days.
- A "Forgot your password?" link sends them a reset email, valid for one hour.
- A "Don't have an account? Sign up" link goes to the sign-up page.
- After 5 failed attempts in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes,
  and the page says so.

After logging in, the member goes back to the page they came from, or to the
home page.
