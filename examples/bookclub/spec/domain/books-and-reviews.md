# Books and reviews

A book has a title, an author, and optionally a cover image.
The same book can be read by many groups.

After a group's meeting about a book, any member who RSVP'd "yes" can review
it: a rating from 1 to 5 stars and an optional text of up to 2,000 characters.
A member can review a book once per group, and can edit their review later.
